A Journey Built on Discipline

Virat Kohli is an Indian international cricketer celebrated for his competitive spirit, batting consistency and commitment to fitness.

From his early days in Delhi to leading India on the international stage, his career has featured memorable run chases, tournament performances and batting milestones.
